===== TABLE 2 (6 rows x 2 cols) =====
Term || Definition
VAT Invoice || This is a China specific term. A Value-Added Tax (VAT) invoice is a document issued by an accountable entity. A VAT invoice sets out the details of a taxable supply and all related information as prescribed by VAT law. In the context of this document ,this is to mention the Golden tax VAT invoice verified with China Golden tax system.
Special VAT Invoice || This is a China specific term. Special VAT Invoices are issued by Trafigura to its counterparty when selling commodities or providing taxable services. The Special VAT invoices are used for VAT deduction purposes. Trafigura also receives Special VAT invoice from counterparty for purchase trades.
General VAT Invoice || This is a China specific term. General VAT invoices cannot be used for VAT deduction purposes. General VAT Invoices are issued by Trafigura to its counterparty when selling commodities or providing tax services. Trafigura also receives General VAT invoice for counterparty for purchase trades.
VAT Invoice Number || This is a China specific term. The golden tax invoice number approved/ generated by Golden Tax system.
Commercial Invoice || A seller-issued invoice that provides detailed information about goods or services sold to a buyer and is used for customs declaration, tax assessment, and international trade settlement. It serves as a commercial and shipping document supporting domestic and cross-border trade transactions. It is also called international invoice in Titan.
