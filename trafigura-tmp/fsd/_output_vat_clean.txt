2. Glossary


For the full glossary of Titan terms the central glossary is maintained here.











Term

| 

Definition

| 




VAT Invoice

| 

This is a China specific term. A Value-Added Tax (VAT)
invoice is a document issued by an accountable entity. A VAT invoice sets out
the details of a taxable supply and all related information as prescribed by
VAT law. In the context of this document ,this is to
mention the Golden tax VAT invoice verified with China Golden tax system.

| 




Special VAT Invoice

| 

This is a China specific term. Special VAT Invoices are
issued by Trafigura to its counterparty when selling commodities or providing
taxable services. The Special VAT invoices are used for VAT deduction
purposes. Trafigura also receives Special VAT invoice
from counterparty for purchase trades.

| 




General VAT Invoice

| 

This is a China specific term. General VAT invoices
cannot be used for VAT deduction purposes. General VAT
Invoices are issued by Trafigura to its counterparty when selling commodities
or providing tax services. Trafigura also receives General VAT invoice for counterparty for
purchase trades.

| 




VAT Invoice Number

| 

This is a China specific term. The golden tax invoice
number approved/ generated by Golden Tax system.

| 




Commercial Invoice

| 

A seller-issued invoice that provides detailed
information about goods or services sold to a buyer and is used for customs
declaration, tax assessment, and international trade settlement. It serves as
a commercial and shipping document supporting domestic and cross-border trade
transactions. It is also called international invoice in Titan.

|

8. Non-functional Requirement


NFR is to capture all the
non-functional processes and system related requirements for effective
deployment and maintenance of the interfaces.












#

| 

Type

| 

Description

| 




NFR-01

| 

Performance

| 

VAT invoice Volumes check Section 5 on page Integration
Options between China TMP and Titan Purchase Invoice & Sales Invoice -
CC&E - Confluence.

China International SI volume: monthly average 190 cargo
& service SI&CN & DN.

The interface should support processing at least 500 VAT
invoices per batch without performance degradation. The batch execution
should be completed within 1 hour under normal operating conditions.

| 




NFR-02

| 

Error Handling and Retry

| 

The interface should provide automatic failure recovery
mechanisms. Failed interface transactions should be retried automatically
every hour.

Users should be able to identify failed invoices and
trigger resubmission where applicable.

| 




NFR-03

| 

Data Integrity

| 

The interface should ensure data accuracy and
completeness throughout the transmission process. No invoice records should
be lost during transmission.

| 




NFR-04

| 

Reliability

| 

The interface should support once-and-only-once
processing. Each invoice should be processed only once for a given interface
transaction without duplication.

Reprocessing of failed invoices should not result in
duplicate invoices.

| 




NFR-05

| 

Availability

| 

The interface should support normal business operations
in China. Planned maintenance windows should be communicated in advance.
Temporary unavailability should not result in invoice data loss.

| 




NFR-06

| 

Audit Trail

| 

For each interface transaction, the following information
should be logged:


Invoice
Number

Invoice
Type

Interface
Execution Time

Sending
System

Receiving
System

Interface
Status

Error
Message (if applicable)

User who
confirmed the invoice for booking


| 




NFR-07

| 

Monitoring and Alerting

| 

The interface should be actively monitored.


Failed
interface executions should generate alerts to the support team in near real-time.

Alerts
should be raised to the support team if no invoices are successfully
transmitted during a scheduled batch run.

Alerts
should be raised to the support team if invoices status is 'Sent'
without final status of 'Issued' ,'IssueFailed' or 'Cancelled' after 5 mins.

Interface
health status should be available for support teams.


| 




NFR-08

| 

Security

| 

The interface should comply with enterprise security
standards.


All API
communications should use encrypted transport protocols.

Only
authorized systems should be allowed to invoke the APIs.

Authentication
credentials should be securely managed and not stored in plain text.

Financial
and tax-related data should be protected during transmission and
storage.


| 




NFR-09

| 

Scalability

| 

The solution should support future business growth.


The
interface should support increasing invoice volumes without major
redesign.

Additional
business line and future China legal entities should be supported
through configuration where feasible.


|
